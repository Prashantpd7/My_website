Preelock

Local-first focus and productivity app for Windows and macOS — make room for deep work.

Built by Prashant Dwivedi (Prashantpd7), Founder of Preelock.

Microsoft Store GitHub Repository Devpost

THE PROBLEM

Staying focused is hard when every app and website is one click away.


Notifications, social feeds, and endless scrolling pull attention away from the work that actually matters, and most focus tools either send your data to the cloud or are easy to escape the moment willpower dips.


The result is fragmented workdays where hours pass with little real progress.

OUR SOLUTION

Preelock was created to solve this problem.

Preelock is a local-first focus app for Windows and macOS. It combines timed focus sessions, app and website blocking, YouTube channel blocking, and screen-time tracking in one desktop app — with everything stored locally, and no cloud or telemetry.


The goal is simple:

Make room for deep work. One intention. One focused session. A little more progress.

KEY FEATURES

Focus Sessions

Start an instant focus session, schedule automatic blocks on a weekly schedule, start a block once at a chosen time, or set daily and weekly time allowances.


App & Website Blocking

Block distracting apps and websites during a session. Blocklists block only the sites you choose; allowlists block everything except the sites you list.


YouTube Channel Blocking

Block all videos and Shorts from a blocked channel — in search, suggestions, subscriptions, and direct links — not just the channel page.


Screen Time

Always-on usage sampling shows where time actually goes, with a weekly chart and per-app and per-website totals.


Camera Presence

Optional camera presence checks keep focus sessions honest, with the camera used only during a session.


Strict Sessions

Strict sessions cannot be paused or ended early, and an unfinished strict session is recovered after a shutdown or restart — the block resumes instead of disappearing.


Local-First

All session data stays on your device. No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry.

TECH STACK

  • Tauri 2 + Rust — desktop app and session engine
  • React 19 + TypeScript — frontend
  • SQLite — local session and usage data
  • Chromium MV3 extension — browser website enforcement

FINAL THOUGHTS

Focus should not depend on willpower alone.


Preelock gives your intentions teeth — blocks that hold, sessions that recover, and a clear picture of where your time goes — while keeping everything on your own device.


The vision is simple:

Build a focus companion that helps you do your best work, one focused session at a time.
